Who it’s for

For small shops with real stock, not monster ERP.

All-G StockMate is built for small retail shops with physical shelves, repeatable stock workflows, and the daily mess of deliveries, batches, dates, and stock movement.

It gives clear answers without turning practical stock work into more spreadsheet archaeology.

  • Food shops
  • Cosmetics shops
  • Household goods shops
  • Stationery shops
  • Specialty shops
  • Independent retailers
  • Small retail teams moving beyond spreadsheets

Daily stock mess

Everyday stock work gets messy when several small checks happen at once.

  • Products arrive before the invoice is checked
  • Expiry dates differ between batches
  • Stock corrections happen after real counts
  • Spreadsheets become another task

What it helps with

Keep your shop stock clear, practical, and usable.

Product database

Manage product names, categories, supplier data, EAN/GTIN, producer codes, and shop-specific details.

Stock levels

Track what is in stock, what is running low, and what needs attention before it becomes retail theatre.

Deliveries and batches

Add deliveries, connect them with products, and keep received stock under better control.

Expiry dates

Handle known dates, missing dates, no date information, or products where expiry dates do not apply.

Stocktaking

Run basic stocktaking, correct stock levels, and make daily stock work more transparent.

CSV import/export

Import product data from CSV files. Export your product base when you need it elsewhere.

Stock movements

Record stock additions, removals, and corrections so everyday inventory changes stay traceable.

Daily filters

Find low stock, missing stock, short expiry dates, expired products, and items that need review.

FAQ

Questions normal people might ask before trusting another piece of software.

What is All-G StockMate?

All-G StockMate is a practical inventory tool for small retail shops. It helps manage products, stock levels, deliveries, batches, expiry dates, stock movements, stocktaking, imports, and exports.

What types of shops is All-G StockMate for?

All-G StockMate is being built for small retail shops that need practical stock control: products, deliveries, batches, expiry dates, low-stock checks, stock movements, and corrections. It can fit shops with physical shelves and repeatable stock workflows, whether they sell food, cosmetics, household goods, stationery, accessories, hobby products, or other retail goods.

What is included in EA Free?

EA Free includes local single-shop use, products, stock levels, deliveries, batches, expiry dates, stock movements, stocktaking, CSV import/export, settings, backups, and up to 2,500 products. It will always stay free.

What is included in Standard?

Standard is planned to add secure local pairing with an Android companion on the same network, product lookup and stock checks, barcode scanning, mobile product creation and editing, invoice photo upload, OCR review, and delivery creation. It will not require a cloud account or provide remote access. Multiple locations and stock transfers belong to Cloud.

Can I import products from CSV?

CSV import/export is included in EA Free. Import product data from CSV and export your product base when you need it elsewhere.

Will All-G StockMate include AI features?

Invoice photo capture and OCR are planned for Standard. Broader AI-assisted product matching and workflow automation remain later-stage ideas. First priority: a reliable stock system that does not need magic to be useful.

How do I download Early Access Free?

Download the signed and approved Windows package directly from this page. Early Access Free does not require an account and runs locally on your device.
